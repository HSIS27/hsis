For students

Who can volunteer?

Different pathways are available depending on your year group.

Years 11–13

Take part in secondary VAPPs, Booster sessions and ECA / club opportunities.

  • Choose from current opportunities
  • Follow the teacher or coach's guidance
  • No previous volunteering experience is required

Years 12–13

In addition to after-school opportunities, Year 12 and 13 students can support Year 7–11 lessons where teacher agreement has been given.

  • Support rather than replace classroom teaching
  • Work alongside the teacher
  • Commit to the agreed session

Volunteer guidance

What is expected?

The programme works best when volunteers are reliable, respectful and ready to support.

Before the session

  • Confirm the opportunity and time
  • Arrive prepared and on time
  • Tell the teacher or programme team if you cannot attend

During the session

  • Follow teacher instructions
  • Support rather than complete work for students
  • Be respectful, responsible and appropriate at all times

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who can volunteer?

After-school secondary volunteering is open to Years 11–13. In-class support is available to Years 12–13 students with teacher agreement.

What if I’m not good or experienced enough?

You do not need to be an expert to volunteer. Choose an opportunity that matches your interests and current strengths, follow the teacher’s guidance, and ask for help when you need it. Volunteering is also a chance to build confidence, communication, teamwork and mentoring experience while supporting others.

How do I choose the right opportunity?

Look for an opportunity that matches your interests, year group and availability. You can use the filters and search bar in the Secondary ECA & VAPP directory, and ask the relevant teacher if you are unsure.

What should I do if I cannot attend?

Let the relevant teacher know as soon as possible if you cannot attend a session you signed up for. Giving notice helps the teacher plan and ensures the support can be organised effectively.

How are my volunteer hours recorded?

After completing a volunteering session, use the hours section on the relevant opportunity to record the time you contributed. Keep your entries accurate so your volunteering record reflects your actual participation.

How do I sign up?

Use the Volunteer form on this website to register your interest in the available opportunities.

Can I volunteer for more than one opportunity?

You can register interest in opportunities that fit your availability and commitments. Final participation depends on the relevant session and teacher or coach.

What happens if I cannot attend?

Let the relevant teacher, coach or programme team know as early as possible so the session can be managed appropriately.

Can teachers request student volunteers?

Yes. Teachers can identify a lesson, Booster, VAPP or ECA where support could be useful and speak with the programme team about the opportunity.

Do volunteers receive certificates?

Certificates will be provided to participating students at a later date.
